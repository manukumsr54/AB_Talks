# 🚀 AB_Talks — Student Hub

> **Everything students need to know. One place.**

AB_Talks is a modern **student information and opportunity platform** designed to help students discover, prioritize, and never miss what matters.

It brings **college announcements, opportunities, deadlines, events, internships, scholarships, and important updates** into one clean, student-focused interface.

Instead of information being scattered across different platforms, WhatsApp groups, notices, and messages, **AB_Talks gives students a single place to understand what they need to know and what they need to do.**

# 💡 Our Thoughtful Idea — "What You Missed"

Students often face **information overload rather than a lack of information**.

Important announcements can easily get buried when students are away from college or simply don't check every platform regularly.

AB_Talks addresses this problem with a dedicated:

> **"While you were away..."**

section.

### 📌 Missed information is grouped into:

🔴 **Important Updates**
Critical announcements and information students should know.

🟡 **Upcoming Deadlines**
Deadlines that require attention soon.

🟢 **New Opportunities**
Recently added hackathons, internships, scholarships, workshops, and competitions.

This allows students to **catch up quickly without scrolling through dozens of old announcements.**

# 🎯 Project Scope

AB_Talks is a **prototype/student project** focused on demonstrating the product concept, interface, and overall user experience.

The following features are intentionally **out of scope** for the current prototype:

* ❌ Authentication
* ❌ Real user accounts
* ❌ Production database
* ❌ Recruiter dashboard
* ❌ Admin panel
* ❌ Production backend
* ❌ Complex enterprise infrastructure

Mock data is used to create a **realistic product experience** while keeping the prototype lightweight and easy to demonstrate.
