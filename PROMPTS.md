# AI Usage Log — AB_Talks

## Project

**AB_Talks — Student Hub**

AB_Talks is a student-focused information and opportunity platform designed to bring college announcements, opportunities, deadlines, events, internships, scholarships, and important updates into one clean interface.

## AI Tools Used

- ChatGPT
- AI assistance was used for brainstorming, UI/UX improvements, React development support, debugging, documentation, and deployment guidance.

## Purpose of AI Usage

AI was used as a development assistant throughout the project. The team used AI to improve the quality and structure of the frontend, solve development issues, refine documentation, and generate ideas for making the student experience cleaner and easier to use.
